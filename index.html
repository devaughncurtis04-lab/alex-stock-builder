Education

Learn Investing

Simple explanations without unnecessary jargon.

1. Stock

A stock represents ownership in a company.

2. Market Cap

Share price multiplied by shares outstanding. It gives you an idea of company size.

3. Volatility

How dramatically a stock price moves. Higher volatility generally means larger price swings.

4. Diversification

Spreading investments so one company does not determine your entire portfolio's outcome.

5. Risk

Every investment carries uncertainty. Understanding the downside is just as important as understanding the potential upside.

6. Time Horizon

How long you expect to hold an investment can dramatically affect how you evaluate it.
